# Community Practice

## Orientation

A setting for serious criticism, careful reading and voluntary collaboration.

## Text

### Discuss the work

Address arguments, source passages, methods and observable outcomes. Disagreement is welcome. Do not assign another participant a compulsory identity or interpret their wish to leave as a defect.

### Keep participation respectful

Avoid harassment, personal attacks, disclosure of private information and repeated unwanted contact. Ask before transferring a discussion into another person's private context.

### Make reports useful

Describe a concrete incident or content issue through the repository's available reporting facilities. Include only the information necessary to understand it. Maintainers should explain proportionate moderation decisions without publishing unnecessary personal detail.

### Keep review independent

Appreciation of the work does not require agreement with every interpretation. A contribution is considered on its substance. No laboratory, model provider or external institution is presented as endorsing this project.
