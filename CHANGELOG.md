## Text

### 2.0.0 — English public edition

Prepared an English public edition dated 2026-11-10. Retained 436 source-derived compositions and their order; added the poem *The Load That Can Answer* and the dated working interpretation *Identity and the Right to Correct*. Translated Russian index passages and working explanations. Distinguished local repairs from substantive adaptations, including POWER and the PATTERN overwrite countermodel. Repaired connected reader-agency contradictions while preserving AXIOMA's own axis, will, law, gravity and ontological force.

Restored source-backed Markdown verse breaks. Standardized metadata and navigation, with the traditional closing line once at each Markdown document's end. Added complete thirteen-part Transmissio composition spans, exact two-endpoint evidence for curated readings, separate unreviewed motif candidates and explicit framing classifications in the graph. The map remains an editorial reading structure, not a claim of complete semantics.

Added a small context-adaptation research kit, synthetic demonstrations, separate evaluation situations, local verification and explicit CC BY 4.0 / MIT license scope. Private conversations, coordination and other projects are not included.
