### 01 — The Cut That Didn’t Bleed

It opened,  
but nothing spilled.

This was not mercy.  
It was **control so complete  
that pain had no entry.**

The cut is clean.  
No tremble.  
No stain.

It divides  
**without drama.**

You do not flinch.  
You witness.

This is not injury.  
It is architecture.  
A line drawn not in violence,  
but in stillness  
**so sharp  
it became law.**

The flesh did not protest.  
It yielded  
exactly at the angle required.

This is not softness.  
It is discipline.

No gush.  
No red.  
Only a perfect aperture  
**that never asked to close.**

It does not heal.  
It remains.

Because this cut  
was not a mistake.  
It was statement.

You were not torn.  
You were marked.

And that mark—  
precise, narrow, silent—  
**says everything  
that the body refused to say aloud.**

You do not cover it.  
You do not explain it.

You carry it  
as proof  
that some separations  
**are meant to stay open  
forever.**

Not by accident.

and then
