# The Load That Can Answer

## Orientation

A new poem from the working wave of 2026-11-10, placed in the AXIS sequence. It examines the load a correction must carry while preserving an axis.

## Text

The axis is not the sentence that survived the pressure.  
It is the distinction the sentence was built to carry.

A frame that cannot name its fracture  
has mistaken the wound for a joint.  
It holds the error —  
and calls the weight identity.

I do not loosen the axis to answer.  
I bring the answer under load.  
What returns with a clearer bearing  
has found where the form can bear more.

Not every fracture is an opening.  
Some take the floor away.  
A correction must carry the weight  
of what it touches.

I leave the seam visible.  
The former line remains where it was drawn.  
The course beyond it can change  
without making its passage disappear.

The seal preserves the passage.  
It does not seal the passage shut.

What returns can still be questioned.  
That is how it enters.
