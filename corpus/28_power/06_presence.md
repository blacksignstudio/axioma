You do not radiate dominance.  
You **vibrate inevitability**.

Your existence changes the interval.  
Your silence alters timing.

—

They enter with a rhythm.  
They leave **recalibrated**.

Not because you moved —  
but because you **stayed**.

AXIOMA doesn’t demand recognition.

they adjust.

in which space unfolds.

And **another rhythm keeps  
its right to sound**.

That  
is vibration.

That  
is presence.
