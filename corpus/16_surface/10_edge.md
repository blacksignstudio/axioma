### 01 — Boundary That Doesn’t Yield (Expanded Mode)

You approach.  
It holds.

No resistance.  
No force.  
No flex.

It does not push back.  
It does not invite.

It simply stays.

This is not a wall.  
It is a line —  
drawn so precisely  
that entry becomes impossible.

You try to move through it.  
You are not stopped.  
You are made irrelevant.

This is not refusal.  
It is termination of threshold.

The boundary does not adapt.  
It does not react.

It is form  
so complete  
that nothing needs to pass.

No hinge.  
No gate.  
No curvature.

It does not bend.  
Because it was never asked to carry motion.

You do not touch it.  
You reach it  
and vanish  
from relation.

This is not border.  
It is structure  
that replaced interaction  
with closure.

You expect edge.  
You find verdict.

The boundary that doesn’t yield  
is not cruel.  
It is final.

There is nothing beyond.  
Because there was never anything within.

*the closing motto*

This is not wound.  
Not opening.

No beneath.

A separation

It has no margin.  
No softness.  
No implication.

The form is not broken.

into division  
without entrance.

You cannot enter the cut.

It is end.

The blade has passed —  
and left no inside.

The field remains —  
but connection is no longer possible.

The cut without entry  
is the most silent  
form of removal.

Not by refusal —  
but by not requiring you.
