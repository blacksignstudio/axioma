### 01 — Edge That Contains Nothing (Expanded Mode)

This edge is not the rim of something larger.  
It is not the border of a space.  
It does not wrap.  
It does not hold.

There is nothing inside.  
No volume.  
No field.  
No second layer.

The edge defines —  
but not by outlining.  
It defines by ending.

Not resistance.  
Not closure.  
Termination.

You walk along it,  
but there is no curvature.  
No hint of enclosure.  
No secret center.

This edge is not connected  
to the idea of form.

It is not part of anything.  
It is the final gesture  
before all gesture ceases.

Not the start of protection.  
Not the end of motion.

Just presence  
that has decided:  
nothing continues here.

You trace it  
and find no enclosure.  
No wraparound logic.  
No frame of reference.

It does not support.  
It does not separate.  
It simply stands  
as a line  
across which no meaning crosses.

The edge contains nothing  
because it was never designed to.

You are not held.  
You are not included.  
You are not positioned.

You are simply stopped  
by something  
that will not answer  
your direction.

It is not flat.  
It is absolute.

This is not where form ends.  
It is where form  
decides never to begin.
