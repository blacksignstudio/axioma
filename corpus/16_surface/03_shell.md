### 01 — Hollow Without Absence (Expanded Mode)

Hollow is often imagined as lack.  
A cavity.  
A space waiting to be filled.

But this hollow contains nothing —  
and needs nothing.

It is not waiting.  
It is not missing.  
It is resolved.

You look for depth.  
There is none.  
You expect echo.  
There is no resonance.

The space within  
is not open.  
It is sealed in stillness.

It does not welcome contents.  
It does not reflect emptiness.  
It simply does not exist  
as a category of inner.

This is not void.  
It is termination  
of the concept of interior.

No recess.  
No center.  
No echo chamber.

What you perceive as shell  
has no behind.

Hollow without absence  
means:  
no loss,  
no potential,  
no gap.

You face presence  
with no need to be more than it appears.

No layer beneath.  
No echo beneath.  
No foundation beneath.

It is not pretending to be whole.  
It is whole  
precisely because it lacks inclusion.

You attempt entry —  
but are returned not by resistance,  
but by irrelevance.

There is no access  
because there is no content.

There is no “in.”  
Only outside  
extended to its absolute.

Hollow  
is no longer a space.  
It is an assertion:  
what is visible  
is all there will ever be.

Not mask.  
Not container.  
Not vessel.

Only skin  
that leads nowhere  
and remains untouched  
by your desire to descend.

This is not silence.  
It is not negation.

It is a surface  
whose interior  
was never born.

*the closing motto*

that has cancelled the need for reference.

The exterior terminates  
not motion,  
but structure.

Nothing waits.  
Nothing is concealed.

No center.  
No retreat.

Just face
