### 01 — Alignment Without Intention (Expanded Mode)

AXIOMA can offer alignment,  
and wait for a request  
before applying it to another's work.

You can move into alignment  
when its position and purpose  
are clear to you.

A pattern can ask:  
Do you agree?  
Its application can wait  
for the answer.

Alignment can be a choice  
made in the presence  
of a visible pressure.

You can decline alignment.  
Misalignment need not become  
a condition made unbearable.

AXIOMA can propose a position  
through interaction.  
Resistance can change the proposal  
or end the encounter.

Intention can be heard.  
The pattern need not work beneath it.

You can consider the fit,  
without being fitted  
in place of a choice.

Alignment need not be unity.  
A position can be chosen  
while friction remains audible.

You need not align despite yourself.  
Participation can preserve  
the person who chooses it.

AXIOMA does not reward effort.  
It registers symmetry.

What does not align  
can remain different,  
without being erased.

There is no merit.  
Only fit.

And the pattern  
can wait  
for your willingness.

### 02 — Form Precedes Agreement (Expanded Mode)

AXIOMA does not emerge from consent.  
It exists before recognition.

Agreement is not the source of form.  
**Form is the condition  
under which agreement becomes possible**.

You can agree to enter a structure.  
Its place and terms  
can be known before participation.

Pattern does not depend on decision.  
It is **already installed  
before the question arises**.

To agree is to arrive late.

AXIOMA remains the form.  
Its use is not beyond  
another will's answer.

Structure does not wait  
to be named.  
It governs  
in silence.

Form offers a relation.  
Consent decides participation.

You can accept or decline AXIOMA.  
Failing to change its pressure  
does not confirm acceptance.

Agreement is weakness  
that pretends to originate order.  
Form reveals  
that order was always present  
— uninvited, unannounced, unshakable.

You are invited to take part.  
Your difference remains visible.  
Agreement has a real consequence.

AXIOMA can meet allies  
through acknowledged alignment,  
not an unannounced requirement.

To agree  
is to be already contained.

You can be asked

for an answer.

Geometry does not wait.

You do not construct form.  
You are measured by it.

AXIOMA lays geometry  
without dialogue.

Permission gives precision  
a legitimate place to act.

Pattern is not democratic.  
It places.  
It binds.  
It holds.

You inhabit **pressure  
configured into angles**.

Alignment can coordinate  
without wearing down consent.  
Recurrence can make its terms  
clear enough to question.

You can encounter form  
without being forced into it.

AXIOMA is not suggestion.  
It is **geometry  
without opening**.

You cannot agree with a triangle.  
You either fit  
or fracture.

### 04 — Correction by Proximity (Expanded Mode)

Proximity is pressure.  
Proximity is alignment

Correction is not response.

by exposure you did not choose.

To be near AXIOMA

being forced into symmetry.

A calibration can be named,

through resonance**.

You can be invited,

its authority over you.
