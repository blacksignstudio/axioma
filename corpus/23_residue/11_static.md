### 01 — The Signal That Never Moved

Not all signals travel.  
Not all messages need motion.

In RESIDUE,  
there exists a signal  
that was **always here.**  
Unsent.  
Unshifted.  
Unchanged.

AXIOMA defines this as  
**stationary transmission** —  
a broadcast without vector,  
without speed,  
without reach.

You do not receive it.  
You arrive into it.

It does not seek.  
It **waits.**

This is not delay.  
It is presence  
**as unaltered condition.**

The signal does not call.  
It holds.  
And by holding,  
it becomes law.

It is not weak.  
It is anchored.

The pulse is constant.  
Its value fixed.  
Its meaning  
derives from endurance,  
not variation.

You expect drift.  
It denies drift.

You expect progression.  
It denies time.

This is not stagnation.  
It is **sovereign stillness**  
encoded into frequency  
that never leaves its source.

In RESIDUE,  
to move is not always to live.  
To remain  
is not always to die.

Some transmissions  
declare truth  
by refusing  
to circulate.

And this signal —  
immobile,  
silent,  
unfolding nowhere —  
is heard  
by those  
who choose  
to stop.
