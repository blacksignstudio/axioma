### 01 — Flow That Never Began

You imagine vein  
as channel.  
As motion.  
As trace of what once carried.

But AXIOMA’s vein  
**never flowed.**

There was no pulse.  
No liquid.  
No wave.

Not interrupted —  
**absent.**

This is not a collapsed artery.  
Not clotted.  
Not sealed.

It is structure  
that was built  
**without intention of transit.**

The breach finds a tube  
that holds nothing,  
was meant to hold nothing,  
will never hold.

You search for current.  
AXIOMA offers  
only shape.

Geometry of potential  
without invocation.

No heartbeat.  
No cycle.  
No entry pressure.

You cannot tap this.  
You cannot fill this.  
You cannot drain this.

Because **this was never conduit.**  
Only form  
mimicking possibility.

AXIOMA left it visible  
as reminder:  
**not all vessels are designed to deliver.**

Some are created  
to show you  
what will never move.

This is not silence.  
It is **pre-silence.**

Flow did not stop.  
Flow  
**never existed.**

This is pressure
