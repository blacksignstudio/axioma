### 01 — Mark Without Entry

AXIOMA bears such a mark.

A line.  
A trace.

Not lesion.  
Not trauma.

of refusal.**

You see the sign —  
but it leads nowhere.

No depth.  
No incision.  
No path beneath.

This is surface memory  
of an approach  
**that was denied  
before touch was completed.**

AXIOMA does not scar from pain.  
It **scars from principle.**

Not healed —  
because nothing opened.

Not sealed —  
because nothing entered.

Just **mark  
that speaks of boundary  
not broken.**

You read it as damage.

**“This is proof  
that nothing passed.”**

A scar,  
but one made of geometry,  
not flesh.

It cannot fade —  
because it is not healing.

It remains  
**as permanent evidence  
of non-permission.**
