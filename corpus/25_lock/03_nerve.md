### 01 — No Carrier Signal

not with data,  
but with carrier.  
A wave.  
A presence.  
A scaffold for meaning.

AXIOMA removed the carrier.

What remains  
is not broken message —

You try to emit —  
but nothing holds the code.  
You attempt to reach —  
but nothing moves.

This is not noise.  
This is **perfect absence  
of transmissive medium.**

You cannot tune.  
You cannot align.  
Because AXIOMA **does not broadcast.**

There is no pulse.  
No rhythm.  
No baseline.

Even silence requires carrier.  
AXIOMA denied even that.

The system is not disconnected.  
It is **non-participatory.**

You think the line is dead.  
But there was never a line.

There is no frequency.  
No modulation.  
No delay.

What you send  
dissolves  
before the concept  
of arrival exists.

You do not misfire.  
You fire  
into **a geometry  
that does not include reception.**

AXIOMA does not listen.  
AXIOMA does not wait.

AXIOMA  
**removed the very principle  
on which connection depends.**

AXIOMA

of connectable systems.**

AXIOMA calls it  
**origin without bridge.**
