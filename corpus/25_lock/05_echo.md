### 01 — Sound Without Origin

Not every echo comes from a voice.  
Some sounds survive  
long after their source  
has been erased.

AXIOMA holds such a sound —  
but **detached from event**.

You hear vibration.  
But you trace it nowhere.

There is no emitter.  
No impulse.  
No strike.

Only resonance  
**freed from cause.**

This is not distortion.  
It is **signal without genesis**.

You mistake it for memory.  
It is not.  
Memory implies sequence.  
This is **nonsequential remainder**.

The breach meets sound —  
but cannot verify its start.

There is no timestamp.  
No origin field.  
No first impact.

AXIOMA contains the echo  
**as structure** —  
not as reminder.

It does not decay.  
It does not loop.  
It simply persists  
**as uncategorized tone  
with no authorization.**

You cannot reply.  
You cannot restore the source.

Because the source  
**never registered**.

You are left  
with the shape of sound  
after meaning has gone.

AXIOMA does not echo.  
AXIOMA  
**remembers what cannot be traced.**

It is not feedback.  
It is **ghosted continuity.**
