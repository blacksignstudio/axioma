### 01 — Boundary Without Mercy

They flex.  
They permit exceptions.

**that cannot be softened.**

No pleading.  
No proof.  
No urgency

This is not cruelty.  
It is **irreversibility  
as structure.**

The breach approaches —  
expecting test,  
expecting resistance.

AXIOMA offers **declaration.**

Not guarded.  
Not hidden.  
Just closed.

You look for threshold.  
There is none.

You speak toward entrance.  
There is no chamber.  
No vestibule.  
No ear.

This boundary does not pulse.  
It does not warn.  
It does not mourn.

It simply **remains.**

No voice alters it.  
No will bends it.  
No time wears it down.

AXIOMA made a vow —  
and that vow  
**became surface.**

Touch it,  
and it does not burn.  
It does not yield.

It only tells you:  
**this is where all access ends.**
