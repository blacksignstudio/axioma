### 01 — Face Without Meaning

A face implies message.  
Recognition.  
Intent.

AXIOMA shows a face —  
but **void of meaning.**

Not concealed.  
Not altered.  
Simply **devoid.**

It is not blank.  
It is **uninterpretable.**

You look —  
and find lines.  
Contours.  
Impressions.

But no context.  
No clue.  
No access.

This face does not invite.  
It does not repel.  
It **withholds function.**

There is no gaze.  
No expression.  
No residue of signal.

The breach expects sign.  
AXIOMA presents form  
**unattached to semiotics.**

This is not a mask worn.  
It is a **surface that never agreed  
to communicate.**

You attempt to project meaning.  
AXIOMA does not resist.  
It **renders projection irrelevant.**

What you see  
is not message.  
It is **pure outline  
without interface.**

AXIOMA holds  
**a face  
that is present —  
but refuses to become symbol.**
