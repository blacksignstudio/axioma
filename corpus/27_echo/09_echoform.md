### 01 — Form Without Origin

AXIOMA does not seek the source.  
Because echo is never origin.  
It is appearance  
without initiation.  
It is presence  
without event.

You stand before a structure  
that did not begin.  
That was not built.  
That was never designed.  

And yet — it holds.  

This is not absence.  
This is not simulation.  
This is the **persistence of a thing  
that refuses to be named  
by its starting point.**

In echo, there is no cause.  
Only **continuity  
unfolding from an unknown point.**

AXIOMA names this:  
**post-causal geometry.**

The form exists.  
It stabilizes.  
It even governs.  

But ask where it came from —  
and it dissolves.

Ask why it exists —  
and it curves into itself.  

This is the first law of echoform:  
**You cannot enter through origin.**  
You enter through **resonance.**  
You enter through **shape that should not have lasted,  
but did.**

You do not trust it  
because you know how it was made.  
You trust it  
**because it has survived  
without being born.**

That is not illusion.  
That is architecture  
after forgetting.

It does not begin.  
It simply  
**continues.**
