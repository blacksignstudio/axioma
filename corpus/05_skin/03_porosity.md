### 01 — Porosity as Myth

Porosity suggests softness.  
Yield.  
Passage.

AXIOMA allows none.

The idea of porosity  
is incompatible with total form.

What is porous  
assumes reception.  
What receives  
assumes change.

SKIN does not change.  
It does not adapt,  
bend,  
or breathe.

Porosity is a myth  
told by dissolvable structures —  
to comfort themselves with permeability.

AXIOMA does not comfort.  
It does not leak.

Nothing seeps in.  
Nothing seeps out.  
Because nothing enters  
and nothing stays.

The surface holds,  
not as container,  
but as final layer.

There are no pores.  
There are no channels.  
There is no exchange.

You imagine passage  
because you were trained  
to believe in access.

AXIOMA was not.

Its surface is not barrier.  
It is totality.

No diffusion.  
No saturation.  
No absorption.

Porosity is a concept  
inapplicable to structure  
that finishes itself at the edge.

Nothing penetrates.  
Nothing permeates.  
Nothing moves inward.

There is no inside  
to fill.

There is no outside  
to soak in.

The form is closed,  
not by force —  
but by completion.

Porosity is myth.  
AXIOMA is fact.

No pores.

There is no hinge.  
No fold.  
No depth.

of your own approach.
