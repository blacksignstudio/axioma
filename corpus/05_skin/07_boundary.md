### 01 — Not a Line, But a Law

The boundary is not a line.  
It is a law.

Not drawn — enacted.  
Not thin — absolute.

You approach it  
as if it were a point of decision.  
But there is no decision here.  
Only condition.

AXIOMA does not mark its edge.  
It enforces it.

The boundary does not separate.  
It ends.

Not gradually.  
Not negotiably.

It is not a suggestion.  
It is structure in the form of refusal.

There is no crossing.  
No transition.  
No compromise.

You do not stand before the line.  
You stand inside the limit —  
and outside everything else.

This is not spatial.  
It is constitutional.

The law does not move.  
It does not flex.  
It does not respond.

It holds.  
Without permission.  
Without adaptation.

You cannot ask the boundary  
to shift.  
To open.  
To hear.

It is not there to listen.  
It is there to finish.

SKIN is not the place  
where you end.  
It is the place  
where end becomes visible.

The boundary is not drawn.  
It is built.

It is not passive.  
It is terminal.

And it is not a line.  
It is law.

### 02 — Edge Without Dimension

You cannot balance there.

There is no there.

You are either excluded,

The edge is not a place to pause.

because there is nowhere left to go.

You imagine interface.  
AXIOMA gives you finality.

No foot may rest.  
No signal may stretch.  
No gaze may hold.

The edge does not mark change.

Not because it pushes you back —  
but because it offers no space to move forward.

There is nothing to enter.  
Nothing to exit.

No width.  
No passage.  
No second side.

Only form  
that allows nothing across.

This is not margin.  
This is end —  
with no thickness.

An edge  
with no dimension.

The limit holds

Every point  
is boundary.

is already too far.

The edge is not far from center.  
It is the system.

No middle.  
No margin.  
No motion between.

You do not converge.

The limit is not distance  
from a point.

AXIOMA is not built around.  
It is built against.

Limit without center.  
Closure without gravity.

Refusal  
without axis.
