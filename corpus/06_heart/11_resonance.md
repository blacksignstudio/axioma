### 01 — Tone Without Feeling

It rings.  
It holds frequency.  
It cuts clean through silence.  
But it carries no emotion.

---

This is not voice.  
Not longing.  
Not release.  
It is tone  
stripped of heat.

---

AXIOMA registers the pitch.  
Stable.  
Unmoving.  
Devoid of color.

---

You expect inflection.  
A shift, a tremor, a hint.  
But this sound  
refuses softness.

---

It is not flat.  
It is exact.  
Too exact  
to feel.

---

You try to mirror it.  
But it offers no echo.  
No space to enter.

---

This is not cruelty.  
It is sound  
without sympathy.

---

The tone is pure.  
And in that purity —  
untouchable.

---

What you hear  
does not move you.  
Because it was never meant  
to move.
