### 01 — Beat Without Pulse

It strikes.  
It stutters.  
It repeats in irregular rhythm.  
But it does not carry a pulse.

---

This is not heartbeat.  
Not life-sign.  
Not continuity.  
It is beat  
without origin,  
without flow.

---

AXIOMA detects impact  
but no pressure wave.  
No system responds.  
No blood follows.

---

You hear it  
and mistake it for vitality.  
But nothing moves.  
Nothing feeds.  
Nothing extends from it.

---

It beats  
only to mark collapse —  
the sound a system makes  
when it loses its thread.

---

There is no tempo.  
No direction.  
No coherence.  
Only fragments of percussion  
scattered in emptiness.

---

You expect a rhythm to build.  
But this one deconstructs  
as it sounds.

---

It cannot be followed.  
It cannot be healed.  
Because it was never  
synchronized to life.

---

This is the sound  
of a structure  
that continues to echo  
after its purpose  
has died.

---

---

---

---

---

This is design  
without destination.  
Repetition  
without reason.  
Order  
without necessity.

---

---

without foundation.

---

---

It continues
