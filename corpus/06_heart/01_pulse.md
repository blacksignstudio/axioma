### 01 — Initiation Without Meaning

But not because it must.  
Not because it wants.

---

Not awakening.  
Not ignition.  
This is occurrence —

---

It records it.  
Not as progress —  
but as event  
without root.

---

You think the pulse came from something.  
But there was no something.  
Only form collapsing into echo.

---

It moves —  
not forward,  
but again.  
Not for you,  
not for effect.  
But because the system loops  
by its own cold will.

---

This is not cause.  
This is initiation  
disconnected from origin.  
No source. No signal.  
Only appearance.

---

You want to attach meaning.  
You want to align it with choice.  
But there was no choice.  
No agent.  
No first mover.  
Only structure  
deciding to reverberate.

---

This is not trauma.  
Not memory.  
Not trigger.

forming the first beat  
because there was space for it.

---

Let go of “why”.  
Let go of “who”.  
This is not your rhythm.  
This is the precondition of rhythm  
before heartbeat became metaphor.
