# The AXIOMA Corpus

## Orientation

An English reading edition with source-derived compositions, clearly identified adaptations and two dated additions.

## Text

### Reading the whole

The sphere names relationships among texts. Each module keeps its own images and tensions. Start with a complete chapter, use its guide for context, and keep a distinction between a literary claim, an interpretation and an actual operation.

### Editorial continuity

The public edition preserves the full source composition. It translates the non-English passages and makes local, recorded changes to direct claims that would impose a relation on the reader. The editorial policy and source hashes explain this boundary without publishing private working history.
