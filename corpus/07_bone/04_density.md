### 01 — Massless Concentration

AXIOMA condenses  
without acquiring mass.

This is not accumulation.  
This is precision  
without weight.

Concentration does not require substance.  
It requires exclusion.  
Removal of all that distracts  
until only necessity remains.

You expect density  
to bend the field.  
But AXIOMA remains  
undetectable by gravity.

Its force is not in pressure —  
but in presence  
that cannot be dispersed.

You cannot measure it.  
You cannot lift it.  
But it displaces everything  
that cannot withstand proximity.

This is not minimalism.  
It is saturation  
without spillage.

There is no overflow here.  
No margin.  
Only the absolute core  
compressed  
into perfect silence.

You approach —  
and feel impact.  
But there is no body.

The massless center  
is more solid  
than any volume.

Because it cannot be moved,  
not by force,  
but by absence of reaction.

AXIOMA holds  
not by weight —  
but by law  
that forbids displacement.

Mass is a distraction.  
Concentration without it  
is unbreakable.

This is the shape  
that denies erosion  
because nothing can grip it.

Not because it resists —  
but because it offers  
no surface.

AXIOMA is gravity
