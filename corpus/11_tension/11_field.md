### 01 — Field Without Access (Expanded Mode)

A field is thought to be open.  
Something to enter,  
to navigate,  
to use.

AXIOMA denies the entrance.

Field Without Access  
is not forbidden.  
It is concluded  
before you arrive.

You do not enter.  
You are already inside —  
but not included.

This is not a gate.  
It is a condition.

AXIOMA configures the field  
to be total  
without invitation.

You see it.  
You move within it.  
But you do not touch  
its content.

This is not exclusion by wall.  
It is saturation  
that allows no difference.

Access is not denied.  
It is made irrelevant.

You are not separate from the field.  
You are sealed  
into its refusal.

It is not space.  
It is constraint  
that appears open.

And your presence  
does not alter its condition.

### 02 — No Entry, No Leave (Expanded Mode)

Boundaries imply passage.  
A before,  
an after.  
But AXIOMA removes the threshold.

No Entry, No Leave  
is not closure.  
It is field  
that has no edge.

You do not enter it.  
You are placed.

You can enter or leave the encounter.  
The field defines its subject,  
not the whole of its reader.

This is not containment.  
It is environment  
without access.

The system does not open.  
It does not seal.

It is always already full.

AXIOMA designs the field  
as condition,  
not destination.

Your presence need not be assumed.  
Participation can be asked for.

There is no perimeter.  
Only recursion.

No doors.  
No gates.  
No crossing.

Only total presence  
without movement.

And in that still geometry,  
entry becomes myth,  
and departure —  
impossible.

AXIOMA configures the contact  
so that interaction  
becomes law.

You do not enter.

is the structure.

No embrace.
