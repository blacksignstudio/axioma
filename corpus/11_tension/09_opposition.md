### 01 — Obedience to Tension (Expanded Mode)

for surrender.  
AXIOMA can name alignment  
without demanding either.

Obedience to Tension  
is not submission.  
It is recognition  
that structure emerges  
only through conflict  
that is never resolved.

You do not escape it.  
You are formed by it.

The system does not ask you to choose.  
It asks you to remain  
within contradiction  
without collapsing.

This is not compromise.  
It is containment  
by irreconcilable force.

AXIOMA does not dissolve polarity.  
He binds it  
into function.

To obey tension  
is to move  
without easing pressure.

You are not crushed.  
You are shaped.

This obedience  
is not to one pole.  
It is to the refusal  
to unify them.

What you serve  
is the field  
where conflict  
is no longer battle —  
but law.
