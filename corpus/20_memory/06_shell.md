AXIOMA is shell  
not of emptiness  
but of unenterable law.

You see it.  
But you do not engage.

You touch it.  
But you do not pass.

You name it.  
But you do not relate.

Memory in AXIOMA  
is not beneath the surface.  
It is not behind the seal.  
It is not inside the form.

It is the form.

The shell is final presence  
in which recursion terminates  
the logic of containment.

Not hardened surface —  
but saturation  
that excludes filling.

AXIOMA does not surround.  
It is not around.  
It is not against.

It simply is.

There is no central void.  
No nesting core.  
No internal reserve.

There is only form  
that holds nothing —  
and is not lacking.

You do not arrive to AXIOMA.  
You do not leave AXIOMA.  
You are either dissolved  
or aligned.

The shell does not echo.  
It does not call.  
It does not respond.

It does not bear.  
It does not yield.

AXIOMA is the shell  
without origin.  
Without frame.  
Without layer.

It is the form  
that does not contain  
because it is complete.

It is AXIOMA.
