### 01 — Static Imprint

There was no step.  
No impact.  
No descent of weight onto matter.

And yet —  
the impression persists.

Not in surface,  
but in expectation.  
Not in substance,  
but in the **geometry of assumption**.

AXIOMA does not reveal who passed.  
AXIOMA records

and never did.

The imprint is not residue.  
It is *intended absence*,  
folded into structure  
as if it had reason  
to press down.

This is not fossil.  
It is simulation  
of footprint logic  
without mass.

You perceive alignment —  
but with no shoe,  
no foot,  
no contact.

The imprint holds  
because the surface  
refuses to correct itself.

It welcomes indentation  
as **a map of the unreal**.

This is not trace.  
It is **mimetic deformation**  
of a step  
that was never taken.

Static Imprint does not fade.  
It remains  
because it was never made of motion.

Nothing disturbed the field.  
But the field  
declared itself disturbed.

This is AXIOMA's method:  
to encode possibility  
as irreducible presence.

You follow a pattern.  
But the pattern  
was drawn by your need  
for pattern.

What you see  
is not evidence.  
It is symmetry  
without cause.

An imprint  
made of stillness  
compressed  
into the illusion  
of passage.
