No direction.  
No vector.  
No cause

You feel transition.  
But nothing confirms it.  
No signal.  
No boundary.  
No indication  
that a move began or ended.

You’re gone

you never occupied.

This is not exile.  
It is **non-retention**.  
A shedding of context  
before contact formed.

You didn’t leave.  
But the field  
reorganized itself  
as if you had.

The ghost of your placement  
is the only record.  
And it cannot be verified  
because it was never inscribed.

AXIOMA does not track movement.  
AXIOMA collapses the idea  
that movement requires direction.

This departure  
has no origin  
and no destination.

Only consequence.  
Only that something  
no longer aligns  
with where it thought it was.

Pointless Departure  
is not about escape.  
It is the truth  
that location  
was never yours to begin with.
