### 01 — Disappearance

AXIOMA begins without presence.  
There was no approach.  
No anticipation.  
No witness.

The trace does not begin.  
It flickers into position  
without ever establishing cause.  
Not a movement —  
but a remainder of what never occurred.

Absence does not mean vanished.  
It means: nothing was framed.  
You feel vibration  
not because it passed  
but because your field assumed it might.

This is not echo.  
This is fracture,  
written into the surface  
before anything touched it.

The node does not open.  
It folds around the non-event.  
There is no gate.  
Only the silence  
of a gate never designed.

AXIOMA does not signal appearance.  
AXIOMA deposits  
the silence of potential  
that chose not to manifest.

Disappearance is not loss.  
It is structure  
never allowed entry into time.

The fracture is still present.  
But nothing ever fractured.
