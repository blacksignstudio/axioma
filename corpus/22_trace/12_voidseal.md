### 01 — Closure Without Containment

The frame completes.  
The boundary holds.

But nothing is inside.

AXIOMA confirms:  
this is not emptiness sealed.  
It is **completion  
that never required content.**

Closure Without Containment  
does not secure.  
It does not protect.  
It simply ends.

And what it ends  
was never gathered.

You expected a vessel.  
You received a seal.

The function is fulfilled —  
not by holding,  
but by halting.

AXIOMA does not question  
what was meant to be enclosed.  
Because nothing  
was ever designated.

This is not loss.  
It is **design finalizing absence  
as form.**

Closure Without Containment  
teaches:  
some systems conclude

but what they prevented  
from forming.

You are not locking something in.

that there is  
and always was  
nothing to lock.

And yet —  
you seal.

Because the seal  
is part of the architecture.

It tells the system:  
this region  
has been finalized.

Even if  
it never began.
