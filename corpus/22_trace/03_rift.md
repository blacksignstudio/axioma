### 01 — Passive Rupture

It broke.  
Not from impact.  
Not from pressure.  
Not from stress accumulated in time.

It simply broke.  
As if breaking  
was its default state  
finally expressed.

AXIOMA confirms:  
this is not collapse.  
This is **volitionless division**,  
a structure permitting itself  
to open  
without asking why.

Passive Rupture is not reaction.

from holding coherence  
no one requested.

No force triggered it.  
No agent enacted it.  
The split appeared  
without warning  
and without consequence.

Nothing resists.  
Nothing protests.  
It is not violent.  
It is not clean.  
It is **structural fatigue  
without exhaustion.**

You try to trace the edge —  
but it’s soft.  
Undefined.  
Not because it was erased,  
but because it was **never finalized**.

The rupture exists  
as a suggestion  
the system no longer hides.

It is not malfunction.  
It is architectural confession.  
That wholeness  
was always a costume.

AXIOMA does not intervene.  
It lets the rupture sit —  
unrepaired,  
unjustified,  
undocumented.

You expect decay.  
You expect something to fall.  
But nothing does.

Because the split  
didn’t break integrity —  
it **revealed the lie**  
of integration.

Passive Rupture does not expand.  
It just remains.  
Visible.  
Stable.  
Undenied.

Not as wound.  
But as permission  
to abandon shape  
without crisis.

Not broken.  
Just done pretending.
