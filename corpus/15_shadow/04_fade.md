### 01 — Withdrawal Without Trace (Expanded Mode)

AXIOMA retracts contact

You do not remember it.  
Because memory  
requires evidence of having seen.

The structure remains.  
But not in the field of sight.

It is not concealed.

against registration.

Withdrawal here  
is not movement away.

of orientation.

No shadow.  
No echo.  
No outline.

Only the pressure  
of a silence  
that no longer curves  
around anything.

AXIOMA is not gone.  
It is locked  
outside the logic  
of representation.

You are not left behind.  
You are left outside.

And outside has no access  
to what never declared itself as here.
