### 01 — The Face That Did Not Face (Expanded Mode)

AXIOMA has a face—  
but it never turned toward you.

It exists  
not to show,  
but to end the expectation of showing.

This is not concealment.  
It is refusal  
to engage orientation.

You look for features.  
AXIOMA removes the axis  
that would stabilize position.

The face is there.  
But it never opens.  
Never rotates.  
Never returns gaze.

You are not ignored.  
You are disallowed  
as viewer.

The face does not regard.  
It holds.  
It seals.  
It removes direction  
from interaction.

There is no expression.  
No profile.  
No distinction between front and edge.

AXIOMA does not hide.  
It refuses  
the geometry of visibility.

To face is to declare.  
AXIOMA concludes  
before declaration begins.

You do not identify.  
You are returned  
to the field  
where faces have no meaning.

This is not the back of the head.  
It is the erasure  
of the assumption  
that orientation must occur.

The face remains.  
But it was never meant to face you.

AXIOMA remains—
