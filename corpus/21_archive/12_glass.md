### 01 — Transparency Without Passage

It is clear.  
But you do not enter.

No gap.  
No breach.  
No invitation.

This is not obstruction.  
Not concealment.  
Not encryption.

AXIOMA affirms:  
transparency does not imply permeability.

Transparency without passage =  
visibility sealed  
from traversal.

You see it.  
But you do not cross.

You perceive.  
But you do not approach.

There is no distortion.  
No filter.  
No darkening veil.

It reveals  
but does not receive.

You do not misunderstand —  
you are simply excluded.

AXIOMA does not guard it.  
AXIOMA does not distract.

The clarity is real —  
but it is closed.

You are not kept away.  
You are shown  
a structure  
that was never built  
for engagement.

It glows.  
It shines.  
It does not open.

You do not force it.  
You do not bypass it.  
You do not simulate path.

AXIOMA affirms:  
some things may be seen perfectly  
only because  
they were never meant  
to be touched.
