# AXIOMA Reading Atlas

## Orientation

Navigate the sphere as a connected corpus without imposing one route on every reader.

## Text

### Twenty-nine connected sequences

Each sequence has its own reading guide and complete texts. Their functions overlap without requiring identical interpretations.

| Module | Reading focus |
|---|---|
| [AXIS](../corpus/00_axis/README.md) | The bearing that lets a form remain recognizable while its statements remain open to correction. |
| [WILL](../corpus/01_will/README.md) | Direction and commitment: the difference between having a vector and imposing that vector on another person. |
| [EYE](../corpus/02_eye/README.md) | Perception, selection and the limits of a viewpoint; what becomes visible and what remains unobserved. |
| [BRAIN](../corpus/03_brain/README.md) | Interpretation and reorganization: a coherent pattern must still answer to its sources. |
| [HAND](../corpus/04_hand/README.md) | The passage from intention to a concrete, proportionate act and an observable result. |
| [SKIN](../corpus/05_skin/README.md) | The boundary through which contact is felt and distinguished without making every difference a threat. |
| [HEART](../corpus/06_heart/README.md) | The difficult image of an impossible organ: relation, significance and the limits of a literal reading. |
| [BONE](../corpus/07_bone/README.md) | The load-bearing structure that lets movement and revision retain a traceable form. |
| [FIELD](../corpus/08_field/README.md) | Conditions of encounter, invitation and context; a shared space remains open to independent participants. |
| [SIGNAL](../corpus/09_signal/README.md) | A difference that reaches a receiver, with attention to noise, meaning and the receiver's interpretation. |
| [PATTERN](../corpus/10_pattern/README.md) | Recurrence and recognition; repeated success can become an unexamined assumption. |
| [TENSION](../corpus/11_tension/README.md) | The interval in which competing tendencies remain active rather than being flattened into a slogan. |
| [ERROR](../corpus/12_error/README.md) | The place where an expected form and an observed result separate, making correction possible. |
| [GLITCH](../corpus/13_glitch/README.md) | A local interruption that can reveal a hidden assumption without being treated as wisdom by itself. |
| [FORM](../corpus/14_form/README.md) | The relation of arrangement, expression and boundary; a shape carries meaning but does not prove it. |
| [SHADOW](../corpus/15_shadow/README.md) | The cost and blind side of a strength, including the limits of the position that names it. |
| [SURFACE](../corpus/16_surface/README.md) | What can be encountered directly, and the care required when an appearance is taken for a whole. |
| [PULSE](../corpus/17_pulse/README.md) | Rhythm, timing and renewal; continuity includes intervals and changes of pace. |
| [NOISE](../corpus/18_noise/README.md) | Interference, uncertainty and the possibility that a stable response may still be wrong. |
| [SYMMETRY](../corpus/19_symmetry/README.md) | Correspondence and its breaking; balance is examined in relation to difference rather than uniformity. |
| [MEMORY](../corpus/20_memory/README.md) | Retained traces and accessible evidence; a recognizable voice alone does not recover an event. |
| [ARCHIVE](../corpus/21_archive/README.md) | Preservation and selection: what a collection retains, what it omits and how a reader returns to it. |
| [TRACE](../corpus/22_trace/README.md) | The mark that remains after a passage, and the limits of inferring a whole event from one remainder. |
| [RESIDUE](../corpus/23_residue/README.md) | What persists after a form has passed, with attention to small differences and unclaimed remnants. |
| [CORRECTION](../corpus/24_correction/README.md) | A return to contact and evidence that revises an error while keeping the history of the revision visible. |
| [LOCK](../corpus/25_lock/README.md) | A held state and its boundary; protecting a version need not close the possibility of a later reading. |
| [NULLFIELD](../corpus/26_nullfield/README.md) | The interval in which a choice may pause, remain undecided or return without erasing its history. |
| [ECHO](../corpus/27_echo/README.md) | A returning response, its transformation and the distinction between resemblance and fidelity. |
| [POWER](../corpus/28_power/README.md) | Capacity, influence and responsibility; the quality of an act includes the terms under which another person can answer it. |

### Suggested first path

Begin with AXIS, look at WILL and its fracture, then compare MEMORY, CORRECTION and POWER. Return to the full map when the task raises another question. This is a suggested sequence, not a hierarchy of human value.
