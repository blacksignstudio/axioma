# Known Limits

## Orientation

A precise scope makes the corpus useful for independent study.

## Text

### Evidence boundary

The repository contains literary work, declared interpretations, synthetic examples and preparation tools. The release checks establish the checks actually run. They do not establish superiority over an ordinary well-written brief or a measured effect on a trained model.

### Context and training

A context packet does not update model weights. Dataset export does not train a model. Training requires a selected base model, its own license and an actual experimental run. No such run is reported here.

### Voice and agency

Literary first-person speech is part of the composition. The sphere and its vocabulary do not provide an empirical measure of consciousness. Readers may interpret or decline a proposed frame. Their actual instructions and environment determine what actions are possible.

### Coverage

Local software checks, sample rendering, actual provider evaluations and independent human reading are different forms of evidence. Consult the [verification record](../verification.json) for observed environments. Hosted CI configuration is not a successful hosted run until that run exists.

### Reuse

The licenses permit their stated forms of reuse; they do not supply rights that a licensor does not own or endorse an adaptation. The release intentionally excludes private archives and other projects.
