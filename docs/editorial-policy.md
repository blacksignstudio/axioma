# Editorial Policy

## Orientation

Preserve the work while making its public reading clear and attributable.

## Text

### Preserve composition

The reading body contains 438 texts in 29 modules: 436 source-derived compositions, the new poem *The Load That Can Answer*, and the new working interpretation *Identity and the Right to Correct*. Source-derived compositions retain their order, section structure and distinctive imagery. Technical uniformity applies to metadata, orientation, section nesting, navigation and the final closing line. It does not make every literary chapter sound alike.

### Name the editorial operation

Local repairs address unmistakable claims that impose participation, deny a reader's agency or describe concealed effects on another person. Some connected passages require substantive adaptation: adding a permission sentence while retaining a surrounding chain of forced submission would leave a contradiction. POWER is a public adaptation, and the PATTERN overwrite chapter is an adapted critical countermodel. These are editorial changes in meaning, not merely spelling corrections.

The edition distinguishes source derivation, translation, local repair, substantive adaptation and new writing in its provenance. Exact preparation changes and preserved originals remain available to the owner outside this public file set. The public repository begins with this attributable edition rather than distributing those private records. A source hash establishes a derivation baseline; it does not prove that two editions have identical meaning.

Ordinary imagery of axis, will, ontological force, self-command, gravity, pressure, law, fracture, persistence and power remains available for philosophical reading. The force of the work is carried by its own form. Another will remains distinct. Preservation of voice is an editorial judgment that readers can question.

### Translate completely

Russian index passages and working explanations are rendered in English. A translation does not become a newly discovered historical original. New interpretations identify themselves by their edition and provenance.

### Keep the public boundary

This repository publishes AXIOMA. Private conversation archives, internal coordination, other projects, local machine paths and unpublished foundation materials are outside its file set. The traditional final motto remains an authorial closing mark. It does not assign a role or action to its reader.

### Keep correction visible

An editorial change should explain its purpose and preserve a reviewable version history. Source hashes identify the preparation baseline without distributing its private files. Contributions can challenge a reading; they do not silently rewrite the history of its creation.
