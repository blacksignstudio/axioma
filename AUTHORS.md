# Authorship and Attribution

## Orientation

AXIOMA is an author-directed project developed with AI assistance.

## Text

### Project credit

Attribute this edition to the AXIOMA project, maintained through the publishing account. The work is author-directed and curated; AI tools assisted drafting, editing, translation, software and review. Assistance does not establish independent human review or external institutional approval.

### Cite an edition

Use the title, edition number and the actual repository address from which you obtained the material. CITATION.cff supplies machine-readable citation metadata. No DOI or laboratory affiliation is claimed.

### Adapt responsibly

Identify modifications and retain the applicable license and credit. A derivative should distinguish its new interpretations and tests from the results recorded by this edition.
